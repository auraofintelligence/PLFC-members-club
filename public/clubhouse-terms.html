COMMITTEE REVIEW · 21 NOVEMBER 2026

Clubhouse terms and etiquette

Demo edition. The committee still needs to adopt the club’s online arrangements before opening them for general use.

Choose how you take part

Your membership gives you access to club dates, published newsletters, member minutes and the results board. You can keep a private fishing journal and choose a cartoon angler for your profile. Joining a social platform is optional.

Under 16. Use the member calendar and junior-friendly club updates. Your angler profile stays private. The club keeps member search, friend requests, shared photos, exact catch pins and the club conversation closed for this age group.

16 and over. Once an officer confirms your age group, you can choose to accept these terms in your member settings. This opens social sharing and lets you submit a measured catch for review. You still choose whether others can find your profile and who can see each shared item.

Respect the people and the place

Family competition entries. A parent or legal guardian aged 18 or over can enter a child who was under 16 for the event. The guardian uses their own member login and can keep separate nicknames for their children. An officer checks the guardian’s authority and the catch photo before verifying the entry.

The guardian can keep the entry between the family and the judging officers, or choose to show the result to signed-in club members. Sharing a nickname and sharing a checked photo are separate choices. A shared result uses “Junior angler” unless the guardian chooses to show the nickname. Choose a fish-only brag mat photo where possible. Ask the child first if they appear in the picture. The guardian can remove the nickname or photo, or withdraw the whole entry. A copy already saved by someone else may still be on their device.

  • Welcome newcomers. Use the same courtesy online that you would show someone beside you on the jetty. Racist abuse, bullying, threats and unwanted contact have no place here.
  • Ask before posting another person’s name, photo, catch or fishing spot. For a photo of a child, ask both the child and their parent or guardian. Respect either person saying no. A fish on a brag mat is usually all the photo needs.
  • Keep a parent, carer or another authorised adult included in club communications between an adult and a child. Use the club’s official channels for club business.
  • Keep someone else’s photo or pin within the audience they chose. Leave school names, addresses, launch routines and children’s whereabouts out of shared pictures and messages.
  • Enter honest measurements. Keep fish handling brief. Give wildlife room and respect local cultural knowledge, including places people choose to keep private.

What the club keeps

An authorised officer records the age group they checked and any relevant guardian permission. The online age check uses a band. Please keep identity documents and birth dates out of it. The club records who made or changed a permission decision, when it happened and which edition of the terms you accepted.

Shared meals. A host can invite particular people or open a meal to eligible club members. A home address is shared with confirmed guests. Keep private invitations and addresses within the party. Other confirmed guests can see what you offer to bring or do. Dietary notes go to the host. Tell the host if your plans change.

Member recipes. Sharing a recipe makes its text, chosen byline and up to four photos visible in the recipe book to signed-in members with sharing access and committee officers. You can edit or remove your own recipe. Withdrawing your sharing agreement hides it from other members. You can download your contributions and recipe photos from My club download.

Friends can open photos or catch pins only while you grant them access. Removing someone or ending a share closes their access in the clubhouse. A file they already saved may still exist on their device. A competition entry is a separate choice. Officers can see the evidence photo and members can see the approved result under the angler name supplied for it.

Keep your contributions

Download your contributions and photos to keep a private copy on your own device. You can do this after withdrawing your sharing agreement or when your membership expires. Save your copy before asking the club to close your login. If your login is already closed, ask the Secretary privately about your remaining records.

Change your mind or get help

Use My access and safety to review your settings or withdraw agreement to sharing. Contact the Secretary, Assistant Secretary or another committee officer in person to correct an age group, change guardian permission, report unwanted contact or ask for a photo to be taken down. If the concern involves an officer, choose another officer you trust. A young member can bring a parent, carer or trusted adult.

For a problem on another platform you can report it there too. Report serious online harm to eSafety.

Why the club uses an under-16 setting

Australia’s minimum age applies to accounts on age-restricted social media platforms. These demo settings are the club’s proposed safeguards. A guardian’s permission to join the fishing club does not open a restricted social media account.
