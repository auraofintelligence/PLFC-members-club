About the club

Point Lookout Fishing Club brings locals, families and visiting anglers together on Minjerribah (North Stradbroke Island). Based at Point Lookout Bowls Club, it puts local fishing meets first. Anglers photograph their catch on a measuring mat so the fish can go back in the water. Beginners are welcome to learn alongside experienced fishers.

Club facts

Club home
Point Lookout Bowls Club, 75 Dickson Way, Point Lookout QLD 4183, opposite Home Beach. Venue details
Fishing together
Regular local meets are the priority. The club is also planning small invitationals with neighbouring clubs. Ask the Secretary for confirmed dates.
How catches count
A photo of the whole fish alongside the marks on a brag mat records its length. This supports catch and release and the club’s approach of keeping only what you need.
