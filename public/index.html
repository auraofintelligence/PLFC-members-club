the local meet.

We’re two years in. The days we want more of are the ones where someone can borrow a rod, learn to bait a hook or catch up with a mate they haven’t seen in a while.

Regular local meets come first. From time to time we’d also like to invite a small group from another fishing club to join us for a friendly fish.

HOW WE FISH

A photo on the mat.
Back in the water.

Our club comps are catch and release. A brag mat is a measuring mat. For an eligible catch, wet the mat, take a quick photo showing the whole fish and the scale, then release the fish. Skip the photo if it needs to go straight back.

Fishing for dinner? Take only what you need and leave fish for the next person.

Who runs the work once the committee agrees?

Choose the task, agree who looks after it, then record the partner’s written acceptance. Until both sides agree, PLFC keeps responsibility.

Volunteer coordination
Ask when a member offers to help with a particular activity. With their permission, the partner can take on rosters and volunteer contact for that activity.
Paid work
Ask when someone wants to explore paid work or an event needs a paid role. The person chooses whether to apply. The prospective employer handles selection, terms and payroll.
Event delivery
Ask when approving the event plan. Delegate a named job, such as staffing the welcome desk. Record who at PLFC owns the event, budget and safety decisions.
