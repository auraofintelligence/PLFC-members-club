PLFC clubhouse demo · Webmaster guide

Connect the club email

The club’s sending address is pointlookoutfc@gmail.com. Members and officers choose their own delivery address in their clubhouse profile. Only the person responsible for the club mailbox needs to complete this setup.

Connect Gmail

  1. Open the club’s Google Cloud project, enable the Gmail API and set up the Google Auth Platform audience and consent screen. For a trial, add the club Gmail address as a test user. For regular delivery, complete the applicable production setup. Google testing grants for sending mail expire after seven days. Open Gmail API setup. Google’s token expiry guide.
  2. Create a web application OAuth client. Add https://developers.google.com/oauthplayground as an authorised redirect URI. Keep the client ID and client secret with the Webmaster. Open Google OAuth clients.
  3. In Google’s OAuth Playground settings, select Use your own OAuth credentials, enter that client ID and secret, and choose offline access. In Step 1 enter openid https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. Select Authorize APIs, choose the club Gmail account and approve. Step 2 gives you a refresh token when you exchange the authorisation code. Open Google OAuth Playground.
  4. In Cloudflare, open Workers & Pages, then plfc-clubhouse, then Settings and Variables and Secrets. Add the three values as secrets named NOTICE_GMAIL_CLIENT_ID, NOTICE_GMAIL_CLIENT_SECRET and NOTICE_GMAIL_REFRESH_TOKEN. Deploy the settings. Cloudflare’s secret settings guide.
  5. Return to your profile. Choose an email option, save and enter the six digits sent to your own address. This checks the club mailbox and your delivery address together. Open my profile.
When the club starts using its own email domain

The Webmaster can verify the club’s new sending domain with Resend, set NOTICE_EMAIL_FROM and RESEND_API_KEY in Cloudflare and retire the Gmail credentials when switching. Members keep their own email choices. Verify a sending domain.
