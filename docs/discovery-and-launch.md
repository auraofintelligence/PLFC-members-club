# PLFC discovery and launch brief

Prepared 20 November 2026 for the new Point Lookout Fishing Club web app. This is an internal working brief. It records evidence and launch decisions without importing private member records, financial information or meeting transcripts into the public website.

## What the app needs to do

Give ordinary club people one place to find the next event, plan a fish, join in and see what needs doing. The experience should feel simple and welcoming on a phone. Familiar language and a few obvious actions matter more than adding every idea from earlier experiments.

The immediate demonstration is for Sunday 22 November 2026 at 10am at the Point Lookout Hotel. The meeting details and the hotel's involvement come from the user. They have not been independently confirmed in a booking or sponsorship agreement. Do not present the hotel as a contracted sponsor.

Regular local fishing meets are the core of the club. Small invitationals give members a way to fish alongside guests from another club. An occasional larger competition is a separate idea for members and the committee to consider once the local calendar takes shape. No frequency, budget, prizes or sponsorship have been agreed for it.

The organiser describes PLFC as about two years old and independent of the former Straddie Classic. That distinction matters when reading older island references. The earlier event supplies neither PLFC history nor a template for the local programme.

## Product areas

| Area | Main job | First useful actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Explain the club and give people a clear next step | See the next event, explore the club, ask about joining |

| Go fishing | Help people plan a local outing | See dated weather and marine information, explore solunar information, open authoritative source links |
| Members | Keep a member's club information together | View membership status, events, notices and communication preferences |
| Committee | Share the work of running the club | Prepare an agenda, record notes and decisions, assign actions, see the next due dates |

Public pages must never depend on understanding the technical systems behind them. The committee experience should make the next action obvious rather than recreate a complex project management tool.

## Findings that should shape the build

- The March 2026 committee record describes an unsustainable administration and technology workload, limited uptake of existing tools, and a need for reminders and shared responsibility.
- A single agreed calendar was still missing. The recorded direction favoured school holiday competitions at a frequency the committee could support, with a committee meeting one to two weeks before each competition.
- Earlier documents contain different proposed competition rhythms and unresolved dates. They are useful background, not current schedules.
- Membership fees and value were under reconsideration in March 2026. Historical forms and posters do not establish current prices.
- The blank membership form separates communication choices, volunteering and photo permission. It also provides guardian consent for under-18 participation. Keep these choices distinct.
- The local constitution revision describes membership admission as a committee decision. A submitted application should remain pending until an authorised person approves it.
- A major marquee competition was already an ambition in the November 2024 sponsor proposal. No confirmed date, prize pool or sponsor agreement was established by this discovery.
- The organiser confirmed that PLFC competitions use brag-mat photographs so people can release their fish and take only what they need. The June 2025 onshore rules poster shows how the club recorded length with the whole fish visible on a PLFC measuring mat. Its entry categories, times and submission channels belonged to that event. Page 3 of the signed 16 July 2025 minutes records a proposal to buy generic mats and add waterproof PLFC stickers. Current stock and any sale price still need confirmation.
- The user has clarified that earlier automation documents vary in quality and are not gospel. Their useful lesson is to reduce duplicated work, not to impose a complicated workflow.

## Launch requirements

### Access and member data

Use three clear permission levels: visitor, member and committee. Committee permissions must be granted by an authorised administrator. Enforce access on the server before private data is returned. A preview mode or a browser role selector is not production authentication.

Keep applications, private documents and member information outside public assets and source files. A member should see their own details and appropriate club notices. Committee access should be limited to what is needed to run the club. Provide a reliable administrator recovery process and a way to remove access when responsibilities change.

### Dates, fees and event claims

Get committee approval for current membership categories, fees, event dates, entry conditions and public sponsor claims. Make proposed events visibly different from confirmed events. Do not fabricate prices, event capacity, prize totals, sponsor logos, testimonials or participation counts.

### Payments

Connect the club's chosen payment account later, after fees and account ownership are settled. Until then, applications and expressions of interest must not imply that money has been collected or membership has been approved. Confirm that payment status and membership status are separate records.

### Email and reminders

Email delivery is not connected by this discovery work. Do not claim that a message or reminder was sent merely because an item was saved. Before enabling delivery, configure the sender, confirm domain settings, test delivery and failure handling, and respect each person's communication choice.

Keep reminders useful and limited. Start with event changes, upcoming meetings and assigned actions. Calendar downloads can work independently of email integration.

### Fishing information

Show provider, location, timezone and update time for weather, tides and solunar information. Explain calculated solunar periods as estimates. Keep tide predictions separate from moon calculations. Do not show invented live conditions when a provider is unavailable. Add the required provider credentials and verify the actual responses before claiming an integration is live.

### Internal event readiness

Insurance was unresolved in the March 2026 record. Check current arrangements before accepting competition entries or publishing coverage claims. This is an internal launch consideration, not homepage copy. Confirm the adopted constitution and relevant event rules before implementing approval or voting automation.

### Cloudflare and the existing domain

Test the app at a preview address first. Confirm hosting, database bindings, access controls, durable storage and backups. Connecting the existing domain requires a reviewed cutover plan that preserves the domain's email records and a way to restore the previous site. The user wants Cloudflare hosting; this brief does not establish that deployment or a domain change has occurred.

## Honest readiness statement

Discovery established useful source material and product direction. It did not verify production sign-in, email delivery, payment collection, live marine integrations, hosting configuration or a domain cutover. The implementation and its test results must state separately what is working, what is a preview and what still needs an account or an approved club decision.

For Sunday's walkthrough, demonstrate a clear public homepage, the next meeting, a useful calendar, a fishing-planning view and simple member and committee experiences. Label demonstration data plainly. Show how an agenda becomes notes and assigned actions, without exposing private source documents. The purpose is to make the club's next steps tangible and easy for people to understand.
