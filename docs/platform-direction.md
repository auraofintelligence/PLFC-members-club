# The platform we are working towards

This demo is for committee discussion. Local members and families should be able to find the next fishing meet, invite a small group from a neighbouring club and help decide what goes on the calendar. An occasional larger competition can be considered when members and the committee feel it fits the island.

PLFC competitions use brag-mat photos to record catch length and allow catch and release. Prepare the measuring mat and camera in advance, minimise handling, and keep only what you need within the fishing rules. A fish that must go straight back takes priority over the picture. [Queensland responsible fishing guidance](https://www.qld.gov.au/recreation/activities/boating-fishing/rec-fishing/responsible-fishing)

## Simple on the surface, substantial underneath

The front door should help a person take the next useful step. The information behind it can grow into a large, controlled library without asking every visitor to navigate an administration system.

A daily briefing should use the day, broad fishing area, method, target and chosen preferences to bring together the relevant forecast, named tide reference, daylight, official rule checks, practical know-how and club activity. Missing or stale data must remain visible. A personalised briefing must never turn a partial rule check or model forecast into a claim that an outing is legal or safe.

The current demo uses selected date, method and target for contextual guidance. It does not use private account information, automatically determine a marine park zone, scrape people’s personal data or provide an AI legal decision. Official rule sources and review dates are kept with the content.

## Guided use

Every meaningful action needs a clear first step and a visible result. The demo includes page guides, a repeatable tour and help beside connected-service choices. Calendar actions should lead to a recognised calendar option with plain instructions, rather than dropping an unexplained file into Downloads.

New member intake, consent, payments, competition entries and committee approvals will need the same guided treatment when implemented.

## Voice first and accountable

The planned meeting flow begins with an announced recording, then transcription, reviewed notes, decisions and named follow-ups. Video is an option. The full service still needs an agreed recording archive, transcription provider, costs, retention and access rules.

Drafts, approved records and public summaries are separate states. Authority is explicit. A published update should carry its source, version, date and approver. Working towards a chosen ISO management standard requires defined procedures, review and recovery as well as software. No certification claim is made by this demo.

## Offline app later

The user has asked for an offline-first app eventually and explicitly deferred starting that work. No offline service worker or app installation is introduced in this stage.

Keep structured records, stable identifiers, content versions and source timestamps so a future app can cache guides and draft records cleanly. That later implementation must distinguish stored forecasts from current conditions, show the last rules review, resolve conflicting edits and confirm authorisation when records synchronise. Private recordings and member information require a deliberate local storage and retention design.

## Integrations only after sign-off

Ready SETCO and the Minjerribah Screen Media Network remain proposed connections. Finnius is the existing all-ages custom GPT and character, with voice and avatar integration deferred. Payments, event reminders, publication approvals and automatic transcription are not live.
